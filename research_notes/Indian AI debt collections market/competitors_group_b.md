## What exactly does each company's product do (workflow, AI voice, human-assisted calling, WhatsApp/SMS/email, analytics)?

### Takeaway
All three sell BFSI-facing communication/automation stacks that touch collections, but they differ in centricity: Rezolv is a dedicated AI-native collections/lending-ops platform; SquadStack is a hybrid AI-voice-plus-human-telecalling CX/sales platform that has a collections use case; Trustt is a broad digital-banking core (lending, distribution, identity) whose collections capability is a dashboard-level module inside its Digital Lending suite, not a standalone collections-calling product.

### Cited Findings
- Rezolv offers AI voice agents operating in 11 Indian languages and a platform spanning "early reminders through legal recovery" for banks/NBFCs, with collection channels across SMS, WhatsApp, email, and IVR — [WebSearch synthesis citing Rezolv coverage](https://pulse2.com/rezolv-raises-12-5-million-series-a-as-ai-debt-collection-platform-handles-12-million-loan-accounts/); [Outlook Money — "Rezolv's disruptive journey from NBFC collections to BFSI customer engagement and recovery"](https://www.outlookmoney.com/spotlight/rezoais-disruptive-journey-from-nbfc-collections-to-bfsi-customer-engagement-and-recovery-8874)
- Rezolv's own funds-use statement says the Series A money will be applied to AI "across sales, risk, underwriting and collections," implying collections is one module within a broader lending-ops suite rather than the sole product — [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2026/08/lend-tech-firm-rezolv-raises-12-million-funding-round-led-by-norwest)
- SquadStack describes a single "operating system" where AI voice agents and human agents work together, centered on an "ROI Optimization Engine" handling leads, data, and performance metrics, with AI handling bulk/routine outreach and humans taking over for empathy/complex negotiation — [SquadStack hybrid CX model](https://www.squadstack.ai/dialedin/hybrid-cx-model); [SquadStack — AI & human collaboration in contact centers](https://www.squadstack.com/innowave/humans-and-ai-will-drive-the-future-of-contact-centers)
- SquadStack's collections-specific page ("Boost Pre-Due Collections," rebranded as "India's Fastest Growing Lending Platform") targets BFSI/lending & credit/insurance/brokerage; it lists a generic "Loan EMI Collections" use case but gives no detailed feature breakdown of a dedicated collections workflow product — [SquadStack boost-pre-due-collections page, fetched](https://www.squadstack.ai/boost-pre-due-collections)

- Trustt's platform (per its own site) has four offerings: AI Banking Voice Agents (20+ Indian languages, live actions like balance checks/card blocks, handoff to humans), Digital Lending (origination, underwriting, disbursal, loan management, and collections on one platform), Digital Distribution (Trustt DDP, for onboarding DSAs/business correspondents/agency networks), and Digital Identity (face auth, Aadhaar eKYC, PAN verification, liveness, bank-statement analysis) — [trustt.com, fetched](https://trustt.com)

## Confirm and detail SquadStack's hybrid AI+human-agent calling workforce model

### Takeaway
Confirmed: SquadStack explicitly markets a hybrid model combining AI voice agents with human telecalling/CX agents, orchestrated through a central platform/"ROI Optimization Engine," with AI handling high-volume routine outreach and humans handling complex, empathy-requiring, or escalated conversations (including, per the company, the humanoid AI handing off to live agents for "real conversations").

### Cited Findings
- SquadStack was founded in 2021 (per search-synthesized company history), originally focused on improving outbound sales through tele-calling, and has since expanded into "an AI-powered platform combining human expertise with advanced automation" — [search synthesis of multiple sources]; note this founding-year claim for "SquadStack" (2021) sits oddly alongside the Entrackr report that the Series A entity was "formerly known as SquadRun" and the Series A itself was reported in September 2020 — suggesting the "2021" founding date may refer to a rebrand/relaunch rather than the company's original incorporation; flagged as a minor chronology inconsistency — [Entrackr](https://entrackr.com/2020/09/chiratae-ventures-leads-series-a-round-squadstack)
- SquadStack's own blog frames the hybrid approach as a unified operating system (not simply "some AI bolted onto some humans"), with a central "ROI Optimization Engine" managing leads, data, and performance metrics across both AI and human agents — [SquadStack hybrid CX model page](https://www.squadstack.ai/dialedin/hybrid-cx-model)
- The division of labor, per SquadStack's own support material and third-party coverage: AI handles first-level/high-volume outreach and routine contact; conversations are routed to human agents when empathy, complex negotiation, or a "real conversation" is needed — [search synthesis of SquadStack voicebot/support pages](https://www.squadstack.ai/voicebot/will-ai-replace-call-center-agents)
- In March 2025, SquadStack launched a customer-support platform built around a "Humanoid AI Agent" as its core, explicitly pitched as blending AI-agent capability with human-like interaction quality — [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2025/03/squadstack-ai-driven-cx-platform-humanoid-ai-agent)
- In August 2025, SquadStack announced a "fully autonomous Humanoid AI Agent Stack" aimed at automating consumer sales workflows — described in coverage as a further step toward AI autonomy within the hybrid model, though it's unclear from available sources whether this reduces or eliminates the human-agent layer for certain workflows — [search synthesis, CMOtech Asia coverage]
- A Delhivery case study (SquadStack-published) attributes 40% lower workforce hiring and engagement costs to combining voice AI with human orchestration — company-published, not independently verified — [search synthesis of SquadStack site]

### Inferences
- The trajectory of SquadStack's own announcements (2021 hybrid platform → March 2025 "Humanoid AI Agent" core → August 2025 "fully autonomous" agent stack) suggests the company is deliberately increasing the AI-to-human ratio over time, potentially moving toward AI-first with human-in-the-loop-as-exception, rather than a fixed 50/50 hybrid split. This is an inference from the pattern of public announcements, not a stated company roadmap.
