# Indian AI Debt-Collections Market — Group A: Credgenics, DPDzero, Gnani.ai, Spocto X

Note on scope: "Spocto X" is the current product/brand name of Spocto, which is **not** affiliated with CRED (the consumer credit-card app) — that is a plausible mix-up given Kunal Shah/CRED's visibility in Indian fintech. Spocto is majority-owned by CredAvenue (now rebranded **Yubi**), a separate company. CRED itself was reportedly one of several investors in CredAvenue's Series A, unrelated to the Spocto acquisition. This distinction is flagged explicitly because sources could easily be conflated.

## What exactly does each company's product do?

### Takeaway
All four sell "full-stack" or multi-channel digital-collections technology to Indian lenders, but they emphasize different cores: Credgenics is the broadest end-to-end suite (digital + calling + field + legal + payments + analytics); DPDzero positions itself as an outcome-based "Collections-as-a-Service" layer (AI voice, telecalling, WhatsApp/SMS/RCS/IVR, field app) that explicitly says it does **not** sell SaaS/CRM software; Gnani.ai is a horizontal conversational-AI/voice-AI company whose collections product, "Collect365," is one vertical application of its broader Voice AI / Inya.ai platform; Spocto X is an "agentic AI" end-to-end debt-collections platform now sold as part of the Yubi Group's institutional lending stack.

### Cited Findings
- Credgenics' product suite (per its own site) covers: automated SMS/WhatsApp/email/voice outreach in 8+ languages plus a GenAI voicebot ("Swara"); a predictive/auto dialer ("DialNext"); a field-collections mobile app ("CG Collect," 22+ Indian languages + Bahasa/Vietnamese, geo-tagging, offline mode); legal/litigation tracking (Section 138 cheque-bounce cases, arbitration, SARFAESI, legal-notice tracking via India Post, online dispute resolution with JCAM); a payments module ("Billzy" — payment links, e-NACH, reconciliation); ML-based risk segmentation/recovery/bounce prediction; outsourced collections servicing; a dedicated ARC product; insurance-renewal product "CredInsure"; and "Prix AI." — [Credgenics.com](https://credgenics.com/)
- Credgenics also offers digital collections analytics, litigation management, a field collections mobile app, and agent performance management, per earlier (2021-2023) coverage. — [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2022/07/credgenics-saas-lenders-loan-recovery-resolution-game)
- DPDzero's platform (per its own site) includes: Voice AI conversations in 8+ Indian languages; "TeleCalling" with DRA-certified human advisors plus AI prompts and 40+ "intelligence signals" on a unified dialer (claims capacity for 20L+/2M+ daily outbound calls); Digital engagement via WhatsApp, SMS, RCS, and IVR; an "Intelligence Layer" for AI-driven risk cohorts and next-best-action; a "Collections Command Centre" for real-time monitoring; a native field-agent (FOS) app with location intelligence/offline access; and a company-level AI model, "DPD-GPT," that segments portfolios and chooses channel/message/timing per borrower. — [DPDzero platform page](https://dpdzero.com/platform)
- DPDzero explicitly offers three service models: Collections-as-a-Service (end-to-end, outcome-based), AI-Led Digital Collections, and Managed Services (running a client's existing collections workforce) — and states it does not sell SaaS or CRM software, positioning itself as an outcome-based service rather than a software vendor. — [DPDzero platform page](https://dpdzero.com/platform)
- DPDzero's WhatsApp channel is claimed to have a 92% message read rate, supports conversational flows capturing payment-promise replies, embeds payment links in chat, and is described (by the company) as RBI-compliant. — [DPDzero product pages, per WebFetch summary]
- Gnani.ai's collections product is "Collect365," built on the company's broader voice-AI stack using ASR (speech recognition), NLU (language understanding), and TTS (speech synthesis) for automated collection calls; the company separately markets a voice-first agent platform called "Inya.ai" that lists debt recovery as one use case alongside customer support and lending. — [Gnani.ai blog](https://www.gnani.ai/resources/blogs/gen-ai-in-banking-automating-debt-collection-with-ai-agents-8278e)
- Gnani.ai claims (self-reported) live conversation response latency under 800ms end-to-end, and cites scale figures of 200+ enterprises, 30M daily calls, and support for 10 Indic languages. — [Gnani.ai](https://www.gnani.ai/?p=44200)
- In February 2026, Gnani.ai partnered with Razorpay so its AI voice agents can generate payment links or UPI requests, track completion status, and confirm settlement during a live call; one report describes the integration as linking Gnani's voice tech to Razorpay's MCP server so software agents can start and monitor transactions in real time. — [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/technology/story/gnaniai-partners-with-razorpay-to-enable-real-time-payment-collections-via-ai-voice-agents-518095-2026-02-26); [The Paypers](https://thepaypers.com/payments/news/gnaniai-and-razorpay-launch-ai-voice-payment-collections-platform)
- Spocto X is described (in a 2025 industry roundup) as "one of the largest end-to-end debt collections platforms" with "Agentic AI" at its core; its press materials describe predictive analytics, dynamic risk profiling, smart automation, hyper-personalized engagement, optimized repayment plans, regulatory-compliant workflows, and early borrower outreach. — [TheWeek.in / PTI wire](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/business/2025/04/03/dcm39-spocto-x.html)
- Spocto is part of the Yubi Group (formerly CredAvenue), which the same release describes as facilitating over ₹1,50,000 crore in debt volumes; Spocto X itself is headquartered partly out of the Dubai International Financial Centre (DIFC) with a MENA presence, suggesting a push into Gulf markets. — [TheWeek.in / PTI wire](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/business/2025/04/03/dcm39-spocto-x.html); [DQ Channels](https://www.dqchannels.com/yubi-announces-international-expansion-spocto)
- Spocto previously ran a "Debt Support and Risk Mitigation Platform" using Movius conversational AI (announced Dec 2022), suggesting the current "agentic AI" / Spocto X branding is a 2024-2025 repositioning of an older product. — [BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20221211005026/en/Movius-Conversational-AI-to-Power-spocto-s-Debt-Support-and-Risk-Mitigation-Platform)

### Inferences
- Credgenics is the most "horizontal/full-suite" of the four, bundling legal/litigation and payments alongside AI collections — a heavier, more complex platform that suggests a longer sales cycle but higher account stickiness once embedded.
- DPDzero's explicit "we are not a SaaS/CRM vendor, we are outcome-based" framing is a deliberate positioning against Credgenics and Spocto X — it is selling a collections *outcome* (often staffing/BPO-adjacent via its own telecalling and field agents), not just software, which could mean thinner gross margins but a different buying motion (easier to greenlight than swapping tech stacks).
- Gnani.ai is structurally different from the other three: it is a general-purpose conversational/voice-AI company (also serving customer support, sales, and other BFSI use cases) for which debt collections is one vertical application rather than the core business. This makes it the most horizontally diversified and the least "collections-specialist" of the group, which cuts both ways — broader R&D base but less collections-specific domain depth than Credgenics/DPDzero.
- Spocto X's 2025 "agentic AI" repositioning and new DIFC/MENA presence suggest the business is being relaunched/internationalized under Yubi ownership, likely to differentiate it after years as a more conventional analytics/outreach vendor.

### Gaps
- No independent (non-vendor) technical review or benchmark of any of the four products' actual AI call quality, voicebot accuracy, or compliance enforcement was found.
- Could not determine if Credgenics', DPDzero's, or Spocto X's "legal" or "litigation" modules are software-only or include actual legal services/panel lawyers.

## What do they claim as competitive differentiation, and how credible/defensible does it look?

### Takeaway
Each company stakes out a different differentiation angle — Credgenics on breadth/full-stack-plus-legal and "India's best-selling platform" awards; DPDzero on being outcome-based/non-SaaS with an AI-first, compliance-embedded model; Gnani.ai on being a horizontal conversational-AI platform with low-latency voice tech and a fresh payments-in-call integration (Razorpay); and Spocto X on "agentic AI" plus Yubi Group's institutional scale and now international (DIFC/MENA) reach — but all four differentiation claims rest substantially on unaudited, vendor-published statistics, and all four face the same structural risk: large bank/NBFC clients appear to multi-source across these vendors, which undercuts any single company's claim to a defensible moat based on client exclusivity.

### Cited Findings
- Credgenics claims to be "India's best-selling loan collections platform," a three-time IBSi Sales League Table winner (award years given inconsistently as 2022-2024 in one place and 2022-2025 in another on its own site), ISO 27001 certified, and reports scale claims of 150+ lenders, 1.7Bn+ omni-channel communications, $250Bn+ in retail loan value touched for collections in FY24, 100Mn+ retail loan accounts managed, 50Mn+ unique borrowers, and 200Mn+ dialer calls — alongside unattributed performance claims of 80% recovery across delinquent accounts, 5X ROI, 20% increase in resolution rates, 60% increase in legal efficiency, 40% reduction in collections cost, and 30% reduction in time to collect. No methodology, client attribution, or time period is given for the performance claims. — [Credgenics.com, per WebFetch]
- DPDzero differentiates explicitly on business model: "Our engagement models are outcome-based" and the company states it does not sell SaaS or CRM software, contrasting itself with "fragmented data and manual escalations" it attributes to traditional agencies; it also emphasizes compliance (TRAI compliance, RBI Fair Practices Code alignment, ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certification) built into every interaction. Self-reported scale/outcome metrics: ₹8,000 Cr "recovered so far" (no time period given), ₹20,000 Cr portfolio managed annually, 2M+ borrower interactions monthly, 35% uplift in collection efficiency, up to 25% reduction in flow rates, and a Moneyview-specific testimonial of 15% reduction in Net Credit Loss. A "70% Conversion Rate" figure appears under every testimonial on the page with no explanation, suggesting it may be a template/placeholder value rather than a real per-client statistic — worth treating with particular skepticism. — [DPDzero.com/platform, per WebFetch]
- Gnani.ai differentiates on being a vertically-integrated, in-house voice-AI stack (owns ASR/NLU/TTS rather than stitching together third-party components, per CEO Ganesh Gopalan's public comments) and on low end-to-end latency (self-reported <800ms); its February 2026 Razorpay partnership (in-call payment link/UPI generation via Razorpay's MCP server) is a genuinely distinctive, verifiable product feature (confirmed by two independent news outlets) rather than a pure vendor-claimed statistic, which gives it slightly more credibility than the pure self-reported numbers. — [Telecomlive interview](https://telecomlive.in/web/?p=1000432); [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/technology/story/gnaniai-partners-with-razorpay-to-enable-real-time-payment-collections-via-ai-voice-agents-518095-2026-02-26); [The Paypers](https://thepaypers.com/payments/news/gnaniai-and-razorpay-launch-ai-voice-payment-collections-platform)
- Spocto X differentiates on "Agentic AI" branding and the backing/scale of the Yubi Group (which it says facilitates ₹1,50,000+ crore in debt volumes), plus a new international (DIFC/MENA) footprint. Its claimed results (25-30% lower bounce rates, up to 57% lower operational costs, 9 crore+ accounts prevented from turning NPA saving ₹50,000+ crore in expected credit losses, and a headline "60% recovery boost" that is never explained or substantiated in the article body) are all vendor claims distributed via a paid PTI/NewsVoir wire release, which explicitly disclaims editorial responsibility for the content — this is a notably lower bar of journalistic scrutiny than ordinary tech-press coverage of the other three companies. — [TheWeek.in / PTI wire](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/business/2025/04/03/dcm39-spocto-x.html)

### Inferences
- Across all four companies, the single most repeated and least-verifiable category of claim is the "X% improvement in recovery/collection efficiency/cost reduction" statistic — none of the four publish methodology, baseline definitions, sample sizes, or named/audited client sources for these numbers, so none of these specific percentage claims should be treated as comparably credible across vendors; they read as marketing copy rather than benchmarked results.
- Gnani.ai's Razorpay integration and its "own full voice stack" positioning are the most concretely differentiated and independently corroborated claims found in this research (two separate outlets independently reported the Razorpay deal with consistent technical detail), making it the single most credible differentiation claim among the four, even though Gnani.ai is otherwise the smallest/least transparent of the four on financials.
- Spocto X's reliance on a paid wire release (rather than ordinary reported coverage) for its highest-profile recent claims (the "60% recovery boost" headline, the ₹50,000 crore savings figure) is a meaningfully weaker evidentiary basis than Credgenics' and DPDzero's self-published-but-at-least-direct website claims, and should be flagged to the report writer as the least independently-scrutinized of the four companies' public claims.
- None of the four companies' differentiation claims directly address the structural risk noted earlier (large banks appear to multi-source across vendors, per overlapping named logos for Credgenics and DPDzero) — none claim or demonstrate single-vendor exclusivity with any major bank, which weakens the durability of any "sticky platform" narrative all four implicitly offer.

### Gaps
- No independent third-party benchmark, regulator statement, or audited case study comparing actual recovery-rate or efficiency outcomes across these four (or against traditional collection agencies/human-only approaches) was found for any of them.
- Could not find any RBI or NBFC-industry-body press commentary specifically naming or evaluating any of these four vendors (the suggested source "RBI/NBFC industry press mentioning these vendors" yielded no results in this research pass) — this is a notable gap given it was an explicitly suggested source category.

## Traction signals (recovery rates, call/account scale, geographic reach)

### Takeaway
All scale/traction figures found are vendor-self-reported (website claims, case studies, or press releases) with no independent verification found for any of the four; Credgenics shows the most financially-grounded traction (actual disclosed revenue and profit figures, which is rarer and more credible than pure operational-metric claims), while the other three rely entirely on unaudited operational claims.

### Cited Findings
- Credgenics: $250Bn+ retail loan value "touched" for collections in FY24; 100Mn+ retail loan accounts managed; 50Mn+ unique borrowers; 1.7Bn+ omni-channel communications sent; 200Mn+ dialer calls; 150+ lender clients; operations extending to Indonesia (per its own site, suggesting early Southeast Asia geographic expansion, consistent with its stated Series B purpose of "SEA expansion"). — [Credgenics.com]; [IBS Intelligence](https://ibsintelligence.com/ibsi-news/credgenics-bags-50m-in-series-b-funding-for-sea-expansion)
- DPDzero: ₹8,000 Cr "recovered so far" (no time window stated); ₹20,000 Cr portfolio managed annually; 2M+ borrower interactions monthly; dialer capacity claimed at 20L+ (2 million+) daily outbound calls. — [DPDzero.com/platform]
- Gnani.ai: 200+ enterprise clients (across all verticals, not collections-specific); 30M daily calls (platform-wide, not collections-specific); support for 10 Indic languages; sub-800ms claimed voice-AI latency; specific collections case-study figures include a "leading auto NBFC" reporting ₹20.03 Cr collected, ₹1.38 Cr OpEx saved, and a 63% collection rate, and a "$6B leading Indian bank" reaching a 55% collection rate across a $1B loan book and 10 crore conversations; an "Indian Bank" case reports 64.80% OpEx saved. — [Gnani.ai case studies, per WebSearch summary]
- Spocto X: claims to have prevented over 9 crore accounts from becoming NPAs, saving ₹50,000+ crore in expected credit losses (no time period specified); Central Bank of India's SMA ratio reportedly fell from 8% (2024) to 3% (February 2025) after adopting the platform; new DIFC (Dubai)/MENA geographic expansion announced. — [TheWeek.in / PTI wire](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/business/2025/04/03/dcm39-spocto-x.html); [DQ Channels](https://www.dqchannels.com/yubi-announces-international-expansion-spocto)
- Credgenics is the only one of the four with independently reported (non-self-published) financial traction: ₹220 crore FY25 revenue (~40% YoY growth) and ₹25 crore profit before tax, reported by Entrackr/YourStory/Entrepreneur India based on company disclosures, rather than purely from the company's own marketing site. — [Entrackr](https://entrackr.com/snippets/credgenics-clocks-rs-200-cr-revenue-and-rs-25-cr-pbt-in-fy25-9357515); [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2025/06/credgenics-clocks-robust-revenue-growth-eyes-acquisitions)

### Inferences
- Credgenics is the only company in this group with traction evidence that goes beyond its own marketing copy (i.e., reported revenue/profit figures that at least passed through independent business-press reporting, even if ultimately sourced from company disclosure) — this makes its traction claims somewhat more credible by proxy than the other three's, which are entirely self-published operational statistics.
- Both Credgenics (Indonesia) and Spocto X (DIFC/MENA) show signs of pursuing international expansion beyond India, while DPDzero and Gnani.ai's public materials (reviewed here) show no equivalent international-expansion signal — suggesting the two more capitalized/established players are the ones currently pursuing geographic diversification.

### Gaps
- No independent, audited, or regulator-sourced recovery-rate or call-volume data exists for any of the four companies; every traction figure in this section should be treated by the report writer as an unverified vendor claim unless otherwise noted.
- Could not determine comparable account-handled or call-volume figures on a like-for-like basis across the four (different companies report different units — loan value, accounts, borrowers, calls, portfolio size — making direct scale comparison impossible from public data alone).
